Alibaba spoils us once again with its open-source releases

### 💎 Qwen3.5 (February 2026)

MoE / Dense architectures, support for 201 languages, 256k context window, multimodality (image + text), plus thinking and search modes (which can be toggled off).

- 🌟 Qwen3.5-397B-A17B — competing directly with GPT-5.2, Claude 4.5 Opus, and Gemini-3 Pro
- 🌟 Qwen3.5-122B-A10B — ideal for enterprise solutions
- 🌟 Qwen3.5-35B-A3B — great fit for local deployment
- 🌟 Qwen3.5-27B, Qwen3.5-4B, Qwen3.5-2B, Qwen3.5-0.8B — dense models

- ⚡️ Models on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/collections/Qwen/qwen35)

### 💎 Qwen3-Embedding & Qwen3-Reranker (released last summer, but still top-tier)

Decoder-based architecture (allowing custom instructions for retrieval tasks), supports 100+ languages. The 8B version ranks #1 on the multilingual MTEB leaderboard. Available in 0.6B, 4B, and 8B variants.

- ⚡️ Embedders on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/collections/Qwen/qwen3-embedding)
- ⚡️ Rerankers on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/collections/Qwen/qwen3-reranker)

- 💎 By the way, Perplexity recently rolled out embedders based on Qwen3-Embedding (0.6B/4B) that outperform the originals. For those interested — check them out on [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/papers/2602.11151).

Everyone is talking about them, but does anyone actually use OpenClaw-like tools? I haven't tried them yet myself, but I try to keep up with the news.

### 💎 Claude Code: Remote Control

Anthropic recently rolled out a [feature](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/remote-control). During a session, you can run `/remote-control` and the session transfers to the mobile app — so you can continue vibecoding when you're away from your computer.

---

### 💎 Claude Cowork: Dispatch

Anthropic created a [competitor to OpenClaw](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13947068-assign-tasks-to-claude-from-anywhere-in-cowork) inside Claude Desktop — you can remotely manage an agent with desktop MCPs, files, tools, and skills. Everything runs within their Cowork, on local hardware.

---

### 💎 CoPaw

Alibaba released their own [open-source alternative](https://github.com/agentscope-ai/CoPaw) to OpenClaw and Cowork. Among the perks — free models are available, including Qwen 3.5. You can use local models via Ollama. It also supports skills.

[MiMo-V2-Pro](https://platform.xiaomimimo.com/#/docs/news/v2-pro-release)

The flagship model: 1 trillion total parameters, 42 billion active. Architecture — MoE + hybrid attention, context window — 1M tokens. Interestingly, just yesterday this model was being tested on OpenRouter under the codename **Hunter Alpha** — everyone thought it was the new DeepSeek V4 😧

Benchmark-wise, it ranks roughly in the top 3 among all models. It's one of the best Chinese models for general tasks, and the absolute best in agentic benchmarks.

Pricing:
- <span>$</span>1 / <span>$</span>3 per million tokens (up to 256K context)
- <span>$</span>2 / <span>$</span>6 (256K–1M context)

---

[MiMo-V2-Omni](https://platform.xiaomimimo.com/#/docs/news/v2-omni-release)

Text, images, video, and audio processed through a unified architecture with dedicated encoders. Model size has not been disclosed. Capable of processing 10+ hours of audio in a single prompt.

Benchmark-wise — it beats Gemini 3 Pro.

Pricing: <span>$</span>0.40 / <span>$</span>2.00 per million tokens.

In the demo, the model autonomously completed an online purchase: found reviews, compared vendors, chatted with support, bargained, and placed the order.

In a second use case: prompted with a single text description, it created a 15-second video across 4 scenes, synthesized audio, fixed a text rendering glitch, uploaded the clip to TikTok, and published it.

---

The models are accessible via API at [platform.xiaomimimo.com](http://platform.xiaomimimo.com/). Access is currently temporarily free.
